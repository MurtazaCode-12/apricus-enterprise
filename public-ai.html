The Problem
Most AI skilling programs don't create jobs
Generic AI courses produce certificates, not capability. They teach everyone the same thing, ignore employment pathways, and leave learners without a way to convert skills into income.

One-size-fits-all doesn't work

A college student, an MSME owner, a working professional, and a government official have completely different AI needs. Training all of them the same way wastes everyone's time.

Certificates without portfolios

Employers don't hire based on course completion certificates. They hire people who can demonstrate what they've built. Most programs test attendance, not competency.

No demand-side mechanism

Skilling without a jobs pipeline is just education. Real impact requires connecting trained talent to employers, freelance markets, and entrepreneurship pathways — not hoping for the best.
